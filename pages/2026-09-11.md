> We are hosting the first in-person meetup of the Grenoble Nix User Group on
> **Friday, September 11, 2026, 4 PM to 6 PM (UTC+2)** at the IMAG auditorium on
> the UGA campus. Hosted by [Yifei Sun](https://github.com/stepbrobd)
> and [Gaétan Lepage](https://github.com/GaetanLepage).

## When

Friday, September 11, 2026, 4 PM to 6 PM (UTC+2).

## Where

Auditorium, espace Colloques, Batiment IMAG, Universite Grenoble Alpes,
150 place du Torrent, Domaine Universitaire, 38401 Saint-Martin-d'Heres.

Getting there: tram line B or C, stop Gabriel Faure. By car, take exit 1
(Domaine Universitaire / Gieres Mayencin) off the Rocade Sud. The
[building access page](https://batiment.imag.fr/public/plan/) has maps, GPS
coordinates, and a campus plan. Older directories list the building under its
former address, 700 avenue Centrale.

The auditorium is one of the seven rooms of the
[espace Colloques](https://batiment.imag.fr/public/planning-imag/) on the ground
floor of the building. It seats 100, including 3 wheelchair-accessible places,
and the whole space is wheelchair accessible. Attendance is free and no
registration is required.

## Program

The session is dedicated to talks and workshops. Newcomers and long-time NixOS
users are equally welcome.

- Talks: short community talks on Nix, NixOS, nixpkgs, and everything else!
- Workshops: hands-on sessions, including help for newcomers getting a first
  configuration up and running.

Sessions will be held in English and French. Bring a laptop if you would like to
follow the workshops. Some food and drinks will be provided free of charge.

There is no registration. If you plan to come, react to the announcement in our
[Matrix room](https://matrix.to/#/#grenug:matrix.org) by Wednesday, September 9,
2026, so we can order the right amount of food.

The auditorium is equipped for videoconferencing, so an online stream may be
available. We will confirm whether it happens, and share the joining details, a
few days before the event.
