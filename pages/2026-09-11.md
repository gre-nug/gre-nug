> The first offline meetup of the Grenoble Nix User Group will be held on
> **Friday, 11 September 2026, 16:00 ~ 18:00 (UTC+2)** at the IMAG auditorium
> on the Grenoble university campus. Hosted by [Yifei Sun](https://github.com/stepbrobd)
> and [Gaétan Lepage](https://github.com/GaetanLepage).

## When

Friday, 11 September 2026, 16:00 ~ 18:00 (UTC+2).

## Where

Auditorium, espace Colloques, Bâtiment IMAG, Université Grenoble Alpes,
150 place du Torrent, Domaine Universitaire, 38401 Saint-Martin-d'Hères.

Getting there: tram line B or C, stop _Gabriel Fauré_. By car, take exit 1
(_Domaine Universitaire / Gières Mayencin_) off the Rocade Sud. See the
[building access page](https://batiment.imag.fr/public/plan/) for maps, GPS
coordinates, and a campus plan. Note that older directories still list the
building under its former address, 700 avenue Centrale.

The auditorium is one of the seven rooms of the
[espace Colloques](https://batiment.imag.fr/public/planning-imag/) on the ground
floor of the building. It seats 100, including 3 wheelchair-accessible places,
and the whole space is wheelchair accessible. Attendance is free and no
registration is required.

## Program

The session is dedicated to talks and workshops. Whether you have been running
NixOS for years or have never evaluated a derivation, there will be something
for you:

- Talks: short community talks on Nix, NixOS, nixpkgs, and everything else!
- Workshops: hands-on sessions, including help for newcomers getting a first
  configuration up and running.

Sessions will be held in English and/or French. Bring a laptop if you would like
to follow the workshops. Some food and drinks will be provided free of charge.

The auditorium is equipped for videoconferencing, so an online stream may be
available. We will confirm whether it happens, and share the joining details, a
few days before the event.
